    What counts as the LLM voice

    Language models write with a recognisable style: hedging (it is important to note, ultimately), stock words (delve, tapestry, robust, leverage, utilize), connector stacking (furthermore, moreover, additionally), formula openers and endings (in today's fast-paced world, in conclusion), vague quantity (a wide range of), balanced pairs (not only, but also), and a flat, even tone that bullets everything and commits to nothing specific. None of these prove a model wrote a text. They show how much of that style is in it.
